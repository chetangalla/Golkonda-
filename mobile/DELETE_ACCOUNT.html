Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your Yatra Tales account and the data attached to it at any time — you don't need the app installed to do this, and you don't need to explain why.

How to request deletion

Email us from the address your account is registered under, with the subject "Delete my account." We'll act on it within 7 days and confirm once it's done.

Email info@yatratales.co.in

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Name, email, phone numberPermanently deleted
Login credentialsAccount deactivated — you'd need to sign up again to use the app afterward
Any active or unused access code tied to your accountDeleted along with the account

Location data is never stored against your account in the first place — it's used live, in the moment, to trigger the right audio, and isn't retained afterward. There's nothing tied to your account on that front to separately delete.

Timeline

Deletion is completed within 7 days of a verified request. You'll get a confirmation email once it's done.
