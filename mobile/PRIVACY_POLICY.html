Privacy Policy

Effective 25 August 2026

Yatra Tales is a guided audio tour app. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and how it's kept — in plain terms, not legal boilerplate you have to decode.

What we collect

Data When Why
Name, email address, phone number When you create an account To identify your account and let you log back in
Precise location While a tour is open, in the foreground To detect when you've reached a tour stop and start the right audio automatically

That's the complete list. We don't collect advertising identifiers, browsing history, or contacts, and the app doesn't use your microphone — the "say yes to confirm you've arrived" prompt during a tour runs on a short timer, not a recording.

Where it's stored

Account details and tour data are stored with Firebase (a Google Cloud service), inside a database only accessible to authenticated Yatra Tales accounts. Editing tour content is further restricted to a small number of approved administrator accounts — a regular visitor account can never gain that access, in the app or otherwise.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data, to anyone, ever.
  • We don't share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We don't use your location for anything beyond triggering the tour audio while a tour is actively open.

The only outside party involved is Firebase/Google Cloud, which stores the data on our behalf and doesn't use it for its own purposes.

Your choices

Location access is optional but required for the GPS-triggered part of a tour to work — you can decline it and still browse the app, though tours won't auto-play. You can revoke location access anytime from your device's app settings.

To review, correct, or delete your account and the data attached to it, email us using the address below — we don't yet have a self-serve deletion option in the app itself, so requests are handled directly.

Children's privacy

Yatra Tales isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the update.
